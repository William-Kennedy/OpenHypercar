About Me - Will Kennedy


I am an student from the UK in YR11, working on my GCSEs. Hobbys include Field Hockey and RC car engineering of all varieties. (link and description below) Projects include various model rockets (one from scratch!), AERORC, Greenpower (both an old car and one from bare chassis), OpenHypercar


Model Rockets

From when I was around 9 to 12 I built and launched a few model rockets. Later when I bought a basic 3D printer I designed my own model rocket from scratch, launched it, and got an immediate buzz for designing and making stuff. The tantilising process of designing and then seeing it in real life is a high I have rode ever since. It's what got me into engineering things, I hope I never stop!


Greenpower

As soon as I joined my secondary school in YR7 I have been attending the Greenpower club. Greenpower is an electric go kart style racing series. Students build cars to race against others. The differance to something like F1 is that the race is for efficiency. Everyone is given the same set of batteries and motor, so its about building a car which goes fast as possible whilst not running out of energy by the end of the race. That means reducing both air resistance and drag from rolling resistance (lightweight) so it's a multi-faceted and exciting engineering challenge. After a year of Greenpower, being the youngest only permitted to do the less important less interesting jobs, I was hooked on motorsport engineering, and its what got me into F1. By the third year though I was effectively leading the club, no one else willing to do it. Nowadays It would be fair to say I run the show, and I love it even more than ever before. Last year we started building a new car from a bar chassis. Its been super challenging to design and manufacture the aluminium suspension and progress is slow with only an hour a week. Nonetheless, Greenpower club has been crucial to my personal development.
