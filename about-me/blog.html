September 14th 2025 - This is the first blog entry as before now I haven't had this website. I have documented a few key moments from before this date on the homepage. Today I drove the car properly. Previously I have only tested the motors staticly and driven the car with a tiny amount of throttle in my living room. Today I attempted to drive the car on an astroturf at my Hockey Club. I chose the hockey pitch as it provides a large flat space with no obstacles, perfect for a stupidly fast car with unkown handling characteristics and an inexperienced driver. Sadly there has been a little rain before hand, maing the surface incredibly slippy. I had the throttle limited to just 50%, and with the trigger about 60-80% in it was ripping the glued tyres off. So better glue is required. Still though, a nice problem to have. In other news I have reset the design from the V4 to make a V5. I felt the highly integrated Aero left room for modularity while only marginally reducing max aerodynamic potential but making it much more accessible, changing designs based on CFD much more easy this way. I have also drawn up some chassis desings I believe will improve stiffness and decrease weight.
